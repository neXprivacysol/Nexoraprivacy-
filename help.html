Help

Answers to the usual questions

Connecting a wallet is where most problems start. Begin there.

How do I connect Phantom?
Install Phantom, create or import a wallet and unlock it. Open the Connect wallet page, press Connect next to Phantom and approve the request in the Phantom window.
How do I connect MetaMask?
MetaMask has to expose a Solana account. Update MetaMask, enable Solana in its settings, then press Connect next to MetaMask. If it shows as found without a Solana account, the version you have does not support Solana yet.
My wallet is installed but shows Not found
Reload the page after unlocking the wallet. If you are viewing Nexora inside a preview frame, open it in its own browser tab, since extensions often cannot reach framed pages.
Which wallets work?
Phantom, MetaMask, Solflare, Backpack, Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet and Exodus, plus any wallet that follows Wallet Standard. Ledger works through Phantom or Solflare.
Does Nexora hold my funds?
No. Your wallet holds the keys and approves every transaction. Nexora never sees a seed phrase or private key.
Why are my balances blank?
This preview does not read the network, so the Vault shows dashes. Balances appear once Nexora is connected to a privacy program.
What is watch-only mode?
Paste a public Solana address on the Connect page to browse without a wallet. A watch-only session cannot sign, so it is safe to use on any device.
Did my transaction go through?
Not in the preview. Shield, Send and Swap walk through each step but never sign or broadcast, so no funds move.
