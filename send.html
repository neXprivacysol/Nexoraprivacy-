Send SOL, USDC or USDT

A real Solana mainnet transfer. Your wallet shows the exact transaction and you approve it there. Nexora never holds your keys or funds.

This is a public transfer. Sender, recipient and amount are visible on the blockchain. Private routing is not enabled yet.

    Network settings
    The free public endpoint is rate limited. Paste your own RPC URL for reliable sends.

    Before you send

    1. Check the address character by character. Transfers cannot be reversed.
    2. Send a small test amount to a new recipient first.
    3. Keep a little SOL for network fees. Sending USDC or USDT to a new recipient also pays a small one-time account fee.
