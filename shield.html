Shield

Move funds into your private balance

Shielding turns a public token balance into a private one. Anyone watching the chain sees a deposit, not what you hold afterwards.

FromNot connected
Preview mode. This form walks through the shield flow but does not sign or send anything.

    What happens

    1. Your wallet approves a deposit of the token you picked.
    2. Nexora builds a private note that only your keys can spend.
    3. The note joins a shared pool with every other depositor.
    4. Your private balance appears in the Vault.
