Features

A public chain, a private wallet

Solana shows every balance and every transfer to anyone who looks. Nexora is built to take that exposure away.

Shielded balances

Hold tokens in a private balance. Observers see deposits and withdrawals, not what you keep.

One-time addresses

Receive on a fresh address each time, so payments to you cannot be grouped under one name.

Private send

Payments split across routes and rejoin at the destination, with no direct line from sender to recipient.

Private swap

Trade from your private balance so the swap is not tied to your wallet.

Selective disclosure

Share a read-only view of your history with an accountant or auditor, and nothing more.

Non-custodial

Your wallet holds the keys. Nexora cannot move your funds and never asks for a seed phrase.
